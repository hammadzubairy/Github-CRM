Your booking page is your best salesperson. It works at 11pm on a Sunday, never puts anyone on hold, and either fills your calendar or quietly sends people to a competitor. After looking at thousands of booking sessions, these are the details that make the difference.

## Show prices and durations up front

Hidden prices are the number one reason people abandon a booking. Show the price and the duration on every service, even if it is a range.

## Fewer than eight services on the first screen

Group long menus into categories. Decision fatigue is real, and a wall of 40 services makes people close the tab.

## Let clients choose a person — or not

Returning clients want *their* stylist or therapist. New clients just want the earliest slot. Offer both: pick a specific team member, or choose **Any available**.

## Show real availability immediately

Every extra click before someone sees a time slot costs you bookings. The best pages show available times on the same screen as the date picker.

## Ask for the minimum

Name, email and phone. That is it. You can collect everything else later.

## Confirm with confidence

A clear confirmation screen with the date, time, address and an add-to-calendar button reduces anxiety and no-shows.

## Make it yours

Your colors, your photos, your tone of voice. A booking page that feels like your brand converts better than a generic form.

Every empty chair or treatment room is revenue you can never get back. Across the studios and clinics on FlowDesk, the average no-show rate before switching was **8.4%**. Three months later it was **3.9%**. Nothing magic happened. They changed three things.

## 1. Confirm instantly, remind twice

A booking that is confirmed within seconds feels real. One that sits in an inbox for a day feels optional. FlowDesk sends a confirmation immediately, then two reminders:

- **24 hours before**, with a one-tap link to reschedule
- **2 hours before**, short and friendly, with the address

The reschedule link matters more than you would expect. Clients who can easily move an appointment rarely ghost it.

## 2. Make the policy visible, not scary

A clear, calm cancellation policy on your booking page sets expectations. Keep it short:

> Life happens. Please give us 24 hours notice if you need to reschedule so we can offer the slot to someone else.

Avoid all-caps warnings and threats of fees on the first visit. Firm but kind wins.

## 3. Watch the pattern, not the person

Most no-shows come from a small group of clients. FlowDesk tracks no-shows per client, so you can spot repeat offenders and require a deposit only for them — instead of punishing everyone.
